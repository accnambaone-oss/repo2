# Direction bible — intentional motion, not decoration

## Every shot has an explicit reason

Use this compact shot contract before animating any scene: (1) question to provoke; (2) new information or emotional shift; (3) exact shape/prop/face receiving focus; (4) framing (wide/medium/insert/close), angle and optical perspective; (5) z layers and focal contrast; (6) exact motion and end state; (7) sound and pause; (8) edit edge/reveal.

Establish spatial geography before abstracting it. Use a low angle to exaggerate the height of a well wall; a top-down plan to reveal confinement and counting; a close insert to expose fear or changed eyes; a tight empty shot for the lone survivor; a wide comparison to expose power hierarchy. Pan only if important information will enter. Zoom only if comprehension or pressure changes.

The user sample is economical illustration. For better storytelling, do not replace its semantic simplicity with indiscriminate particles, lens flares or random camera moves. Every glow should correspond to a threatening object, point of curiosity, rescue possibility or information hierarchy.

## Retention architecture for 90-600 seconds

0-2s: immediate dissonance, irreversible event or question. 2-10s: clear stakes, with a withheld cause. 10-40%: successive discoveries and reversals; every beat explains one thing while opening one question. 40-70%: human/societal consequence; reveal counterevidence or second-order effect. ~70%: reinterpretation/twist (the story meant something else). 70-95%: constructive and destructive applications and personal relevance. Final seconds: concrete agency, callback to first image, end-credit sources if factual. For 4-10 minute topics use chapter acts with distinct questions rather than repeating suspense hooks.

Adaptive duration formula: use spoken narration time as the floor, then add meaningful visual silence for 0.2-1.8s before or after major revelations. Never inflate a 3-minute story to 10 minutes just because the range allows it.

## Sound

Voice should remain clearly in front of ambient sound. Let the scene sound arrive ~0-3 frames before the focal event for anticipation. Use subdued rumble for the well, sparse attacks for scarcity, an almost silent beat at TRAPPED, dry impact for control diagrams, airy harmonic resolution for the final exit. Procedural synthesized bed in `scripts/audio.py` is an original placeholder foundation; make bespoke sound cues for your chosen narrative.

## Text and safety

Burned captions and titles compete for attention. Keep titles brief and legible, captions 3-7 words, one emphasis at a time, color only for urgency/cause/effect. A forced aligner or manual captions pass is essential for publication-grade word sync. Keep credits legible for minimum 2.5-3 seconds each, depending on amount of text. Do not imply that illustrative numbers are measured research data.
