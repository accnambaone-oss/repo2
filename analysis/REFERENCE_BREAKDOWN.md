## Story engine (why the video retains attention)

1. **Puzzle**: dead pig and dead well, promising an event without disclosing why.
2. **Reward**: food seems free.
3. **Irreversible reversal**: the same architecture that provides food prevents escape.
4. **Escalating consequence**: hunger → conflict → elimination.
5. **Strange survivor**: a lone red-eyed rat transforms the threat.
6. **False ending**: release looks like resolution; it is not.
7. **Second revelation**: the farmer's actions acquire a larger meaning.
8. **Abstraction**: human hierarchy, strings, chessboard demonstrate the same relational structure.
9. **Actionable payoff**: stop fighting the other trapped players; seek the exit.

This two-stage interpretation (literal physical trap → abstract social trap) is the video's signature. The top headline functions as a semantic bridge between micro-shots, not simply a label.
